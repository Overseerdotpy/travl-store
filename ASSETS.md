# Assets and licensing

The **code** in this repository is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).

The **media** is not covered by that license. Please don't reuse it without asking.

| Asset | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/clips/press.mp4` | A short clip of a clear mechanical switch being pressed, generated with Kling and re-encoded with ffmpeg (H.264, a keyframe every 8 frames so it can be scrubbed smoothly). |
| `assets/images/*.jpg` | Still frames taken from other Kling-generated clips of the TRAVL keyboard. `esc.jpg` is a crop of the terracotta Esc key. |
| `assets/wordmark.json` | The TRAVL wordmark as SVG paths, derived from Montserrat Bold outlines (see `tools/make_wordmark.py`). |

TRAVL is a fictional brand. Nothing on the site is a real product, and no order is ever charged or shipped.

## Fonts

Montserrat and DM Mono are loaded from Google Fonts at runtime. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
